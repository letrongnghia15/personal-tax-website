Privacy Policy

How Sea Tax Services collects, uses, protects, and shares your personal and tax information.

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Sea Tax Services ("we," "us") prepares tax returns and provides tax, bookkeeping, and payroll services. Federal law treats tax preparers as financial institutions, so we are subject to the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, the FTC Privacy Rule, and the FTC Safeguards Rule. We are also bound by Internal Revenue Code §7216 and §6713, which restrict how tax preparers may use and disclose tax return information. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

Information we collect

  • Through this website: the information you type into our contact or intake forms, such as your name, email, phone, city and state, filing status, the types of income and deductions you expect, business details, and any notes you write.
  • When you become a client: information needed to prepare your return, such as Social Security numbers or ITINs, dates of birth, income documents (W-2s, 1099s, K-1s), bank account details for refunds or payments, prior-year returns, and IRS correspondence. We do not collect this information through the website forms, and we ask you not to enter it there.
  • Automatically: our web host and font provider may receive standard technical data such as your IP address and browser type when you load a page. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule a consultation.
  • To prepare, review, and e-file your tax returns and related forms.
  • To represent you before the IRS or state agencies when you authorize us to.
  • To provide bookkeeping, payroll, and tax planning services you request.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory, and recordkeeping obligations.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing. We disclose information only:

  • To the IRS and state tax agencies when filing your returns or representing you.
  • To service providers that help us run the practice, such as our professional tax software, e-file transmitter, secure document-sharing service, payment processor, email provider, and website form provider (Web3Forms, which delivers form submissions to us). These providers may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • As required by law, such as in response to a court order or subpoena.
  • With your written consent. Under IRC §7216, we will not use or disclose your tax return information for any other purpose without a signed consent form that meets IRS requirements.

How we protect your information

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect client information, consistent with the FTC Safeguards Rule and IRS Publication 4557 (Safeguarding Taxpayer Data). These include access controls, multi-factor authentication on systems that hold client data, encryption, and secure methods for exchanging documents. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please never send Social Security numbers, account numbers, or tax documents by regular email or through this website's forms. We will give you a secure way to share them.

How long we keep information

We keep client tax records for as long as required by law and professional standards (generally at least three years after a return is filed, and longer when needed). Inquiries from people who do not become clients are deleted when they are no longer needed. When we dispose of records, we do so securely.

Your choices and rights

  • You may ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information you sent us through this website, subject to our legal retention obligations.
  • Because we do not share your information with outside parties for their own marketing, there is nothing you need to opt out of.
  • To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Data breach notification

If a security incident affects your information, we will notify you and the appropriate authorities (including the IRS, the FTC, and the Washington State Attorney General where required) as required by law.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them through the site. Information about dependents is collected only from a parent or guardian as part of preparing a return.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. Existing clients will receive any notice required by law.
